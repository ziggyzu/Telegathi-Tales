About Us

Telegathi Tales

Telegathi Tales is an intimate music and storytelling platform born in Khulna, Bangladesh—created to bring artists and music lovers closer together in an environment built around music, conversation, creativity, and connection.

Through intimate live sessions, acoustic performances, musical collaborations, artist conversations, and thoughtfully produced visual content, we create a space where emerging voices can be heard, established artists can connect, and audiences can experience music beyond the stage.

Starting from a small room in Khulna, our vision is to grow into a community and creative platform that celebrates the people, stories, and sounds shaping Bangladesh's music culture.

Come for the music. Stay for the stories.
